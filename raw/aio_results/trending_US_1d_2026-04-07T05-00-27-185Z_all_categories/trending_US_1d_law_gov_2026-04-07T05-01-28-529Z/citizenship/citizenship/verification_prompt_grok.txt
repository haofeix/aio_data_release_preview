Home » How to Become a U.S. Citizen Through NaturalizationImmigration Topics ExplainedBecoming a U.S. citizen is a major milestone. For most immigrants, this happens through a process called naturalization.This guide explains what it means to become a U.S. citizen, the main paths to citizenship, and how naturalization works. You’ll also learn about eligibility requirements, timelines, and the fee involved. The goal is to help you understand the process clearly before you decide to apply.Key TakeawaysMost immigrants gain citizenship through naturalization after getting a green card.You must meet residency, good moral character, and English and civics requirements.The process includes filing Form N-400, attending an interview, and taking the Oath of Allegiance.Naturalization requires filing fees, but some applicants qualify for a reduced fee or waiver.U.S. citizenship provides permanent status and important rights, including voting.What It Means to Become a U.S. CitizenU.S. citizenship gives you the highest legal status in the United States. Citizens can vote in federal elections, apply for certain government jobs, and petition more family members to immigrate. Citizenship also provides permanent security in the United States, meaning your right to live and remain in the country cannot be taken away under normal circumstances.U.S. citizenship also comes with responsibilities.
