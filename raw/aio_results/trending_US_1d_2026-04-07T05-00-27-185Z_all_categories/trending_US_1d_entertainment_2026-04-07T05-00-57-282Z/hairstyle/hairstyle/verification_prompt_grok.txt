Wait … back to side part.For all the group-chat discourse, TikTok hot takes and millennial existential crises that the hair-parting pendulum spurred, it overlooked a major truth: that wearing your hair down doesn’t need to be such a dichotomy. A lot of us stick to our go-to look day after day — so much so that the suggestion to shift our part by a couple inches feels radical. But what if this spring, instead of debating which part is back “in,” we just … switched up our hair style regularly?To be fair, there’s a reason we find ourselves locked in on a signature look: “If you’ve got a set schedule and you know you can do the same old, same old in 25 minutes and it will look the way you expect it to, there isn’t great motivation to try something new,” says Toronto-based hairstylist Ashley Readings.Aside from the good ol’ fun of experimenting with hairstyles (like the joy of wearing a new outfit, but without the hit to your credit card bill), there’s a protective factor too. “A lot of the damage our hair suffers is from repetition,” says Readings. “Many people struggle to see hair growth around their face-framing layers because they focus their hot tools on those pieces and if they don’t change their part or style, they get damaged more than the rest.” ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW Powered By 10960.7K 68What's your desert island movie?ShareNextStay To get you started, we put together a week’s worth of different ways to wear your hair down, with tips from Readings to get it done easily and quickly.
