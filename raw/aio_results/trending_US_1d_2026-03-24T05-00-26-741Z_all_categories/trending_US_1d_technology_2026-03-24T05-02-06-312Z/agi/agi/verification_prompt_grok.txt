More specifically, artificial general intelligence refers to systems with broad, flexible and transferable intelligence that don't require task‑specific programming.Artificial general intelligence (AGI) is distinct from the broader category of AI. Nearly all AI in use today — including systems built with machine learning — excels through specialization and pattern recognition rather than general reasoning.AGI, by contrast, implies general‑purpose intelligence. An AGI system would be able to understand tasks in context, transfer knowledge between domains and apply reasoning to situations it has never faced before.
