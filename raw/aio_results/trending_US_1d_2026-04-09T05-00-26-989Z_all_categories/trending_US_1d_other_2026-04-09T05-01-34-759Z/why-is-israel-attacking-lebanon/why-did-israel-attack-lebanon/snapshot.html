    Key reasons for the attacks include: 
    • Targeting Hezbollah: The IDF stated they are focusing on destroying Hezbollah infrastructure, carrying out over 100 strikes in short periods to cripple the organization's ability to fire rockets into Israel.
    • Protecting Northern Israel: The operation aims to establish security for Israeli communities near the border, which have faced consistent rocket fire.
    • Ignoring the Iran Truce: Israel and the U.S. stated that the ceasefire agreement with Iran did not apply to operations against Hezbollah in Lebanon, allowing them to continue their military campaign.
    • Geopolitical Strategy: The attacks are part of a wider effort to weaken Iranian influence in the region, with Israel expanding its air and ground actions against Hezbollah. 
    This action is part of the ongoing 2026 Lebanon war, which has led to significant casualties and displaced over one million people.
