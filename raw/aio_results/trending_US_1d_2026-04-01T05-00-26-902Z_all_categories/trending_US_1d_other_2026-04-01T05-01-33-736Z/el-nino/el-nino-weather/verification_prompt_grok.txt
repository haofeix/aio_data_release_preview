Here is a description of the categories and criteria they use. Watch: Issued when conditions are favorable for the development of El Niño or La Niña conditions within the next six months. Advisory: Issued when El Niño or La Niña conditions are observed and expected to continue. Final advisory: Issued after El Niño or La Niña conditions have ended.
