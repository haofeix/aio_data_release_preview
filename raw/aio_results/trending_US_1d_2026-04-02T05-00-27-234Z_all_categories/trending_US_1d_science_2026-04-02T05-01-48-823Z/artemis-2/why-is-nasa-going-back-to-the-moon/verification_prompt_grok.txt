On Sept. 12 1962, President John F. On Wednesday night, NASA successfully launched Artemis II — America’s first crewed lunar spaceflight in more than half a century. Its mission is clear-cut: Send four astronauts around the moon and back in 10 days.But Artemis II’s mission is also … familiar. In 1968, three Apollo 8 astronauts circled the moon without landing, then traveled back to Earth.In other words, NASA already pulled off a version of Artemis II nearly 60 years ago — and did so without the long delays that plagued Artemis II itself, which was previously scheduled to lift off, and then delayed, almost every year since 2021.How can going to the moon be so difficult if we already did it?
