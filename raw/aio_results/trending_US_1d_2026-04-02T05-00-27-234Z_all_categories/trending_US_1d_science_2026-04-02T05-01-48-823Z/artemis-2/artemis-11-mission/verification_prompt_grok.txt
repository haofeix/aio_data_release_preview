“We are 41,541 miles from the moon,” says astronaut Reid Wiseman, commander of Artemis II, NASA’s upcoming mission returning to the moon. There’s a lot of pressure on Wiseman and his fellow astronauts. They are laying the groundwork for an effort will land astronauts on the lunar surface for the first time in over 50 years.But there’s no tension in Wiseman’s voice right now.
