She is a quiet Supe with dangerous telekinetic abilities. However, she would be used as a test subject for Vought International in order to create a stabilized version of Compound V for adults, which was successful, transforming her into a Supe and granting her superhuman powers and abilities. The Boys Series[] The Boys: Season 2[] "The Bloody Doors Off"[] Mother's Milk, Frenchie, and Kimiko successfully infiltrate the Sage Grove Center security room and notice Cindy sitting idly in the middle of her cell on the security cameras. Following the confrontation between Frenchie and Lamplighter, which results in Cindy's cell door being burned down, she exits the cell and is advised to remain calm by Lamplighter, who reminds her of all the good he has done for her, even assuring Cindy that he likes her. She asks Lamplighter about his friends, but Mother's Milk informs her that they aren't his friends and even proposes that she kill Lamplighter. Cindy tells Mother's Milk that they're dressed like his friends and that she doesn't like liars; however, before they can answer, Cindy is shot in the back numerous times, then kills her attacker with her powers, giving the others time to escape. After that, Cindy utilizes her powers to destroy the other cell doors, freeing the other patients. In a hallway, Cindy searches for an exit but is confronted with a gas canister, which she promptly destroys. Before she can discover who threw it, Stormfront arrives and electrocutes Cindy, knocking her out. Sometime later, Cindy hitchhikes along a road, having completely escaped from Sage Grove Center. Seven on 7 with Cameron Coleman[] During the October 2021 airing of Seven on 7 with Cameron Coleman, a sketch of Cindy is displayed, and it is reported that she is wanted in connection to several violent crimes. As a result, Vought International has offered The Seven's assistance to bring her to justice, with Black Noir offering to deal with the situation as quickly and quietly as possible. The Boys: Season 4[] "Assassination Run"[] Sometime after Cindy hitchhikes away from Sage Grove Center, she would be amnestied and recruited into Vought's security personnel as a deputized agent. Cindy is seen dropping a ship in the middle of the road while Hughie Campbell and Annie January are attempting to flee the country. This causes Hughie to be arrested by Vought security personnel. Cindy attempts to kill Annie but Annie flies away to escape before Cindy is able to use her powers. The Boys: Season 5[] "Fifteen Inches of Sheer Dynamite"[] Cindy is one of two Supes stationed at Freedom Camp 47, where Hughie, Mother's Milk, Frenchie, and other civilians are being held. From a guard tower, she uses her powers to kill a fleeing inmate, much to the disgust of Hughie and Ivy, who watch in horror. At night, Cindy is blinded by Starlight during their mission to rescue The Boys. As the inmates begin to rebel, Cindy prepares to kill them, but Butcher stops her and uses his tendrils to throw her from the guard tower into the barracks. Dazed, Cindy tries to recover, but Butcher restrains her again and lifts her into the air with his tendrils. Before he can kill her, Homelander intervenes with his heat vision, forcing Butcher to release her. Cindy takes the opportunity to escape as Butcher and Homelander begin to fight. "Every One of You Sons of Bitches"[] Cindy is among the group of Supes led by The Deep sent to find Stan Edgar at his underground bunker. When they eventually gain access, Cindy finds Hughie who unbeknownst to her, is fighting an invisible Maverick. She stalks into the room and uses her telekinetic powers to violently explode -a figure, unbeknowst to her she accidentally killed Maverick. Annie enters and seeing the bloody aftermath, promptly breaks neck, killing her instantly. Powers and Abilities[] Powers[] Telekinesis: Cindy throwing a ship in the middle of the street Cindy possessed telekinetic abilities, allowing her to move objects and crush people with her mind. Her powers required the use of her hands, which she moved to direct and control the objects or individuals she targets. Cindy's telekinesis was powerful enough to lift a fishing boat, crush metal doors, and violently cause humans to explode. Superhuman Strength: Cindy possessed superhuman strength, which made her stronger than humans. The full extent of Cindy's strength is unknown. Superhuman Durability: Cindy unfazed by gunshots Cindy possessed superhuman durability, making her more durable than humans and most Supes. She was fully bulletproof, taking repeated gunfire without injury. Cindy was also capable of surviving Stormfront's electricity and being hurled into a building by Butcher's tendrils. Superhuman Hearing: Cindy possessed superhuman hearing, allowing her to hear better than humans. Like all Supes, she could hear higher frequencies that humans cannot.[1] Weaknesses[] Biological[] Godolkin Virus: As a Supe, Cindy was vulnerable to the Godolkin Virus, which would target the Compound V in her bloodstream, neutralizing her powers and killing her within seconds. Hand Usage Required: Like Kenji, Cindy was required to clench her hands in order to properly activate her powers, so if her hands were restricted or broken, she was rendered powerless. Butcher exploits this weakness by immediately restraining Cindy's hands with his tendrils before she can attack him. Brute Force/Limited Durability: Despite being more durable than humans, Cindy could still be stunned, knocked unconscious, and killed by extreme force. Stormfront knocked Cindy unconscious by electrocuting her. Additionally, Butcher easily overpowers Cindy with his tendrils and nearly kills her, but Homelander stops him. She was ultimately killed by getting her neck snapped by Starlight. High Frequencies: Due to being a Supe, Cindy was susceptible to high frequencies. When a high frequency is playing, it could induce immense pain and temporarily neutralize her. Trivia[] Cindy is likely a parody of Netflix's Eleven from Stranger Things.
