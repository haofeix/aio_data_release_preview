Federal income tax was briefly introduced with the Revenue Act of 1861 to help fund the Civil War, and subsequently repealed, re-adopted, and held unconstitutional. The early taxes were based on assessments, not voluntary tax returns. Tax payment dates varied by act.[4] The Supreme Court decided that the Act's unapportioned income taxes on interest, dividends, and rents were effectively direct taxes.
