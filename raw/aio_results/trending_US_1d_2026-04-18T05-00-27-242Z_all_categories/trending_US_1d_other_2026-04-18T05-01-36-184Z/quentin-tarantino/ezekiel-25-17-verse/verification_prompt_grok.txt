Where can I find the exact reference in Ezekiel 25:17 that Jules Winnfield in Pulp Fiction quotes?Answered by · Upvoted by , MBA Movies, University of California, Santa Cruz · Author has 1.2K answers and 41.1M answer views · Mar 4, 2024 · Let’s be clear, you cannot find the Jules Winfield quote from Ezekiel 25:17 in the actual Bible. While Ezekiel 25:17 is very much a real Book, chapter and verse, it doesn’t say what Jules Winfield says on screen. Quentin Tarantino took “artistic liberties,” so to speak, when he drafted Jules’ “badass” speech. While the speech sounds suitably fire and brimstone, Tarantino was looking for the Bible to say something it didn’t. He was looking for it to boil a number of prominent Judeo-Christian themes down to a single memorable quote, in a way which the Bible never actually does.(Jules Winfield, a profane, cold-blooded hitman who has the perfect Bible quote for his killings? Cool for the film, but not really real.) When Tarantino couldn’t find an apt verse he took a real passage that said a very small bit of what he wanted it to say, pulled it out of context and greatly expanded and altered it to incorporate all the themes he wanted Jules Winfield to be pondering in the film. It was kind of a brilliant technique. Many viewers to this day believe actor Samuel L Jackson is quoting the actual Bible when he isn’t.(It turns out Jules Winfield isn’t really religious, at all.
