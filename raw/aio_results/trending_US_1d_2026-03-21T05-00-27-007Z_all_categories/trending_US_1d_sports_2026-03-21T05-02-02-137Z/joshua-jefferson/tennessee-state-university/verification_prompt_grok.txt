Davis between 1943 and 1968. In 1979, the University of Tennessee at Nashville merged into Tennessee State due to a court mandate.[11] The merger was part of the settlement of the Geier vs. Tennessee law suit.[13] In 2022, Tennessee State University was awarded $250 million from the state legislature.
