It’s not stagnant, though; it grows and contracts as lenders price in perceived risk. Federal Reserve decisions: The Fed doesn’t directly control mortgage rates, but their decisions can have a trickle-down effect. Mortgage lenders pay close attention to what the Fed says and does, and may respond by raising or lowering rates after Fed decisions. The global economy: Global trade issues, from conflicts to tariffs, can impact the decisions investors make. If this leads to buying or selling Treasury bonds, it will move mortgage rates.
