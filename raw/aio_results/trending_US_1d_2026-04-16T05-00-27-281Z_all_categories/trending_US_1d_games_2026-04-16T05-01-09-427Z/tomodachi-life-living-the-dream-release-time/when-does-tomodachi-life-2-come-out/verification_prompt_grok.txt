This article is about relationship statuses between Miis. For more information on a romantic relationship between two Miis, see sweetheart or spouse. An example of relationship statuses in Tomodachi Life. When Miis become friends, sweethearts, spouses, etc., their relationship statuses can be checked by pressing L or R while inside their apartment. Different statuses have different colors, which range from: Dark green (Highest) Green Lime green Orange (Default) Red Lavender Purple (Lowest) The higher a Mii's status is with another Mii, the more likely they will become best friends, confess their feelings, and propose. On the other hand, the lower a Mii's status is with another Mii, the more likely they will fight or breakup (if their relationship is romantic). How well a Mii gets along with another depends on their personality, with those in the same group getting along easier, while Miis in different personality groups will have slightly harder relationships. Contents 1 Raising or Lowering Relationships 2 List of Statuses 2.1 Tomodachi Life (North America) 2.2 Tomodachi Life (Europe) 2.3 Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream 3 Trivia 4 Crush 5 Gallery 5.1 Tomodachi Life Raising or Lowering Relationships[] Main article: Problems/Relationships Occasionally, a Mii will ask the player to judge the Mii's relationship with another islander. If they are at Getting along OK or lower, the Mii is a little worried about their current relationship with their friend, sweetheart, or spouse. The player can tell them that they are either great together, okay together, or not compatible. The relationship status respectively will either go up, stay the same, or go down. A Mii's relationship with others depends on the amount of times they spend with each other. Visiting and spending time with each other will raise their relationship, while not visiting each other much will lower their relationship. Miis with a high relationship will visit each other more often, while those with a low relationship won't visit each other as much. When Miis are hanging out in an apartment together, they may engage in conversations. If their conversation does not go well, it may become an argument, which will lower their relationship level faster. If the Miis end up fighting, their relationship status will have a question mark next to it (indicating dodgy feelings) and the player may have to resolve a fight between both of them. If a fight fails to be resolved, they can potentially be removed from the respective relationship list and may revert back to acquaintances (or strangers in Living the Dream), but there's a chance they may become friends or fight again. If the Miis were in a romantic relationship when this happens, this may lead to them breaking up. When Miis go on vacations together via the travel ticket, their status will go up by one or two levels automatically. If a Mii takes their sweetheart/spouse on a date, then the relationships of both will increase. When a Mii confesses to their crush and gets rejected, the statuses of both the Mii and the crush will decrease by up to 3 levels. If they try to confess again and still fail, the status will not go down further. If a Mii asks the player if they should break up with their sweetheart, and the player tells them to work things out and they successfully do so, then their relationship statuses will reset to the default level with each other if they were negative. However, the same does not happen to spouses who ask for a divorce; their relationship status will remain the same until one of the possible scenarios described above happens. List of Statuses[] There are multiple status categories when it comes to relationships: Spouse Family Siblings Sweetheart Best Friend Friends Ex-sweethearts/spouses Strangely, grandparents, uncle/aunt, cousins, and in-law relatives are not listed as Family. Instead, it is up to child Miis and grandparents to make friends with each other. Different categories will have different descriptions for relationship levels. If a Mii's "relationship to the real life you" preferences were set, they will not date the lookalike Mii and will fight them less often. This won't be possible if the game starts up with several phone calls waiting to be answered. In the Japanese and Korean versions of Tomodachi Life, the relationship colors are different from the Western releases as shown below, or a reverse: Magenta (Highest) Orange Amber Green (Default) Dark green Teal Dark blue (Lowest) If Miis interrupt a confession and the target picks one of the love rivals instead of the confessor, the status between the confessor and the target will not lower. However, it will lower if either the target rejects them all, or if the player picks "Bad idea" when the target picks a love rival. Crush[] Introduced in Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream, a Crush is sometimes created when two Miis meet, with the gender of the crush determined based on the Mii's set dating preferences. Upon this happening, a cutscene plays and romantic music plays as the background goes to pink with the Mii stating that they might be in love with such Mii. The player can choose between "You're probably imagining things" and "I think it's love!". If the same mii falls for a different Mii, then the player must choose which one they should go after. When a Mii sees their crush in the open, they will blush and a synth will play until the crush walks away. If the player attempts the drag the mii to their crush, they will sometimes hesitate and admire from afar.
