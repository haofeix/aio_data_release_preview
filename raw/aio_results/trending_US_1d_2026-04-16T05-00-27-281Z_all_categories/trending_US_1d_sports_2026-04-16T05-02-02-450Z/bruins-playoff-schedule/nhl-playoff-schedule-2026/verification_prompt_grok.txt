In the first round, the lower seeded wild card in the conference will play against the division winner with the best record while the other wild card plays against the other division winner, and both wild cards are de facto #4 seeds. The other series will match the second and third-place teams from the divisions. In the first two rounds, home-ice advantage is awarded to the team with the better seed. Thereafter, it is awarded to the team that had the better regular season record.
