Early life and high school career[edit] Betts was born in Vitoria-Gasteiz, Spain and moved around the country during her childhood due to the basketball career of her father, Andrew.[1] Before focusing on basketball, she also played soccer.[2] Betts played basketball for Grandview High School in Aurora, Colorado.
