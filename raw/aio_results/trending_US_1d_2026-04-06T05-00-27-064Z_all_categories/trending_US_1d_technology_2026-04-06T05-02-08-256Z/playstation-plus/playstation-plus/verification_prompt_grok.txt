PlayStation's long-running subscription service, PlayStation Plus (or PS Plus for short) has been through a lot of iterations since it was first introduced all the way back during the PlayStation 3 era. Certain things have stayed the same, like subscribers getting a few games a month added to their library with the price of their subscription, but most of it has changed, and this hub is designed to keep track of those changes. Below, you'll find lists of every game available at each tier of PS Plus subscription, including breakdowns of the different tiers and their prices. We'll also keep everything as updated as possible, as we do with our Xbox Game Pass Games Hub. Table of ContentsPS Plus Tiers (Pricing, Features)PlayStation Plus Essential - $9.99/monthPlayStation Plus Extra - $14.99/monthPlayStation Plus Premium - $17.99/monthPlayStation Plus Games (by Tier)PS Plus Essential GamesPS Plus Extra GamesPS Plus Premium Games (Classics Catalog) PS Plus Tiers (Pricing, Features) There are three subscription tiers to choose from with PS Plus. NOTE: All prices are in USD, and pricing may vary based on your region. PlayStation Plus Essential - $9.99/month Beginning with the lowest tier, PS Plus Essential is your foot in the door. It's what you get if you're not looking to have a Game Pass-style library of games, and just want to be able to play online with your friends. For $9.99/month, a PS Plus Essential subscription gets you: Monthly Games - anywhere from three to four games that you can add to your library each month, and once you add them to your library, you'll always be able to go back and play them so long as you have an active subscription Online Multiplayer - the ability to play multiplayer games online and take advantage of any game's online capabilities Exclusive Discounts - discounts on games in the PlayStation Store not available to non-subscribers Cloud Storage - the ability to keep your game save data backed up in the cloud, making it extremely easy to transfer from one console to another Share Play - the ability to invite your friends to play a game on your console by digitally passing the controller to them, or vice-versa Exclusive Content - select free-to-play games and first-party PlayStation games will sometimes include cosmetic items/DLC only available to subscribers One thing worth noting with the monthly games is that you must manually add them to your library while they are available. Each monthly batch is only available to add to your library for a limited time, and if you miss the window, you miss out on getting those games through your subscription. Next up is PS Plus Extra, the middle-of-the-road tier that, in terms of what you get for an extra $5 a month, is actually a pretty significant increase in value. That's because the main different between PS Plus Extra and PS Plus Essential is a massive library of games to play, Game Pass style, with more added every month (and, of course, some removed). Here's what a PS Plus Extra subscription gets you: Everything that comes with PS Plus Essential Game Catalog - a library of games that span across first-party and third-party titles, all playable through your subscription, with more added each month, anywhere from six to eight games Ubisoft+ Classics - a library of classic Ubisoft titles to go on top of the library of games included in the Game Catalog It's worth noting that, unlike the Monthly Games you get as part of the Essential tier, when a new batch of games is added to the Extra-tier Game Catalog, you don't have to then go manually add them to your library to access them after the month they are introduced.
