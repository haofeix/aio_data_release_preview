Celebrations of Eid al-Fitr typically last for three days, one day fewer than those of Eid al-Adha. According to the Quran, the Prophet Abraham (Ibrahim) was willing to sacrifice his son to Allah, but Allah accepted the sacrifice of an animal instead. Eid al-Adha, or the Feast of the Sacrifice, celebrates Abraham’s willingness to sacrifice his son for God.Celebrations of the holiday typically last for four days, and are similar to those of Eid al-Fitr, except that Muslims celebrating Eid al-Adha traditionally acknowledge the occasion by slaughtering an animal for meat.
