Nebraska's head coach comments on his squad leaving spring.We're not to April yet but Nebraska spring football is complete.That doesn't mean anyone is about to hit the pause button and go take a nap."The great thing about this year is now we have five, six weeks now here of training and work," Matt Rhule said after Saturday's Spring Game. "Anyone that's banged up can get healthy. More importantly we've seen these guys now. We know what they do well, we know what they need to improve on ... So happy with the spring."‌Nebraska beat the Huskers 22-17 if you like an official score attached to your spring scrimmages.Freshman running back Jamal Rule had 119 yards on nine carries. Linebacker Derek Wacker had a game-high 10 tackles. Jacory Barney had six catches for 43 yards. Kickers were 4-of-4 on field goals. The attendance was 27,188.‌"I'm grateful for everyone that took their time to come out here and be a part of a good day. It sends a great message to our players, it sends a great message to the recruits. We have really an amazing group of recruits," said Rhule, who passed on his congratulations to Nebraska's men's basketball team for their Sweet 16 run. "But more importantly just come together and celebrate our state, celebrate our university, celebrate the football team and all the hard work that we've done."Here's everything the Husker head coach had to say.How much was today a sampling of Anthony Colandrea's ability to escape the pocket and fit some tight throws?"Yeah, he's done that all spring. As has TJ. As has Danny really, and Bode. I think when you see one guy doing it (it) kind of become contagious. It's so hard on a defense, especially quarters-based defense where you're always kind of playing three-over-two and you're doubling receivers. It's a great way to neutralize it." Some of those runs were off zone reads where there's also a throw option. "But a lot of them are just in dropback. I know in the two-minute before the half he was yelling at me because he felt like he wouldn't have been tackled when I blew the whistle. But yeah, he's got a unique gift. And he gets outside. Then I think our receivers recognize that. They're fighting to get open. Even the one, it was a naked but he got out there and he kind of bought time.
