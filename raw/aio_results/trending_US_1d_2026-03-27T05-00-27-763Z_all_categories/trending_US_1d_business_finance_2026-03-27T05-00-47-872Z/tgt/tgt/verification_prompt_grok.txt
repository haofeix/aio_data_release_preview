Meaning & Significance of TGT Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT) describes a professional who has completed a graduation (bachelor’s degree) in a relevant subject along with a certification in teaching such as B.Ed (Bachelor of Education). TGTs are directly involved in imparting subject-based education to adolescent students, preparing lesson plans, evaluating assignments, and creating a supportive classroom environment. TGT is not a separate course but a title/designation granted to those who hold the necessary qualifications and training to teach classes 6 to 10.
