Early life and education[edit] Golden is from Phoenix, Arizona, and graduated from Sunnyslope High School in 2003.[1] Golden played basketball and baseball at Sunnyslope and helped them win the 2002 Arizona 4A state championship in basketball, scoring 6 of their 62 points in a 62–61 victory.[2] In 2003, Golden enrolled at Saint Mary's College of California, where he played guard for the Saint Mary's Gaels from 2004 to 2008.
