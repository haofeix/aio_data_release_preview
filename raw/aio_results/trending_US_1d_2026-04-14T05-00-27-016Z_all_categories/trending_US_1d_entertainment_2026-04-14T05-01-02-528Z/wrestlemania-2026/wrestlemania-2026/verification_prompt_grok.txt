Night 1 received largely negative reviews, with commentators citing the short match times (particularly for women's matches), heavy prevalence of ads, celebrity involvement, and lack of grandeur. Kevin Berge of Bleacher Report gave Night 1 a grade of D+[87] Although he praised the unsanctioned match between Jacob Fatu and Drew McIntyre and the match between Seth Rollins and Gunther, Robert Jackman of Yahoo Sports called Night 1 "miserable" and "uninspired," opining that it had fallen short even of the low bar set by the widely-panned main event of WrestleMania 41 Night 2.[88] The main event match between Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton for the Undisputed WWE Championship was universally panned. Wade Keller of Pro Wrestling Torch considered the match as taking "forever to get out of first gear", but when it did, the match "was dramatic, but hardly athletic". However, "the McAfee situation just muddied the story of the match" while stating that "it's hard to separate fan fatigue with Cody from just the effects of years of lousy, uneven booking of him".[89] Shakiel Mahjouri of CBS Sports gave it a grade of C+, stated that WWE "washed their hands of the celebrity nonsense, leaned into speculation about Orton's health and built towards an electrifying finish", but after McAfee showed up, "everything imploded". McAfee counting the pinfall and Orton turning on him "made no sense and completely took attention away from what was shaping up to be a great match".[90] Jordan Mendoza of USA Today called it a "frustrating and confusing" main event and a "really odd end to night 1."[91] In contrast, Night 2 received a positive fan and critical reception, with the main event match between Reigns and Punk for the World Heavyweight Championship receiving widespread critical acclaim. Dave Meltzer of Wrestling Observer Newsletter said on his podcast that it was "one of the best matches in Wrestlemania history."[92] Scott Carlson of WhatCulture called the main event an "incredible" match with "great action and brilliant pacing," ending "a really good, solid show".[93] Contrasting his negative Night 1 review, Kevin Berge gave the event an A- grade, with particular praise given to the main event and the Oba Femi vs Brock Lesnar match.[94] The Raw after WrestleMania opened with Oba Femi, who entered to a massive ovation.
