The first Indy car events in the Dallas–Fort Worth area were held at Arlington Downs from 1947 through 1950, sanctioned by the American Automobile Association (AAA) Contest Board.[1] From 1997 through 2023, Speedway Motorsports-owned Texas Motor Speedway (TMS) in Fort Worth hosted events under IndyCar sanction. The event is billed as a partnership between IndyCar, the Cowboys and Rangers, and the City of Arlington.[3] The Arlington street circuit was designed by Tony Cottman, who has designed several Champ Car and IndyCar street circuits.
