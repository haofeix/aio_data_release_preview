On July 2, 2021, the NCAA changed its rule to allow its athletes to earn money from their name, image, and likeness (NIL).[51] Dunne was projected to earn more than any other collegiate athlete due to her large social-media platform, which was a combined five million followers across both Instagram and TikTok at the time of the rule change.[52] In August, Dunne announced that she signed with WME Sports, becoming their first NIL athlete.[53] A month later, she announced her first exclusive brand partnership with activewear brand Vuori.[54] As of May 2023, Dunne was the highest-valued women's college athlete with an estimated NIL valuation of $3.3 million,[55] which led to endorsement deals with Grubhub, Vuori, Bodyarmor, and American Eagle Outfitters.
