To be fair, only a handful of Oscar contenders have ever had the opportunity to lose so many times. (The previous record, split five ways, was 11 losses, set by 1948’s Johnny Belinda and tied by 1964’s Becket, 1977’s The Turning Point, 1985’s The Color Purple, and 2021’s The Power of the Dog.) And, at least mathematically, Sinners having more nominations than any movie in history made it more vulnerable too.
