Despite delivering two standout performances as identical twin brothers Smoke and Stack in the Warner Bros. horror film, Jordan isn't eligible to submit for Best Actor and Best Supporting Actor. That’s because, when it comes to acting nominations, the Oscars recognize the individual, not the individual roles. In other words, Jordan can only be nominated once per film, in either category — but not both. This restriction dates back to a unique moment in Academy Award history.
