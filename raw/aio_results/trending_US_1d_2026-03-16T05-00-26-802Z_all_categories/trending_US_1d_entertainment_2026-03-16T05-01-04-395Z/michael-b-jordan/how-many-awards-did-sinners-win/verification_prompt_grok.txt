After a few words from Seftel, Gloria Cazares, whose daughter Jackie was shot and killed in Uvalde, Texas, at her elementary school in 2022, stepped to the microphone.“My daughter Jackie was 9 years old when she was killed in Uvalde,” said Cazares. “Since that day, her bedroom has been frozen in time.
