If this is less than the number of applicants, a cutoff time will be required. Finally, runners are lined up according to how many seconds they are under their qualifying time, commonly called their buffer. Then, simply find the point at which only the accepted number of applicants is beneath the cutoff time. This year, that was 4:34.
