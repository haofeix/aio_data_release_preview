Young was voted PGA Tour Rookie of the Year for the 2021–22 season on the strength of five runner-up finishes, including the 2022 Open Championship. He attended Fordham Preparatory School in The Bronx, where he was a member of the golf team.[3] At age 15, Young shot a 2-under 70 at James Baird State Park Golf Course to win the Catholic High School Athletic Association New York State Championship.[4] Young chose to attend Wake Forest University, where he studied Economics, graduating in 2019.[5] He received the Lanny Wadkins Scholarship at Wake Forest.
