Rory McIlroy will take home $4.5m after successfully defending the Green Jacket at Augusta to win his sixth major. That’s $300,000 more than the Northern Irishman banked when he completed the career Grand Slam last April. A brief history of the Masters prize money When the Masters was founded in 1934, Bobby Jones and Clifford Roberts were keen to attract as strong a field as possible. So they put up a $5,000 purse with $1,500 of that going to the winner – around $36,000 in today’s money.
