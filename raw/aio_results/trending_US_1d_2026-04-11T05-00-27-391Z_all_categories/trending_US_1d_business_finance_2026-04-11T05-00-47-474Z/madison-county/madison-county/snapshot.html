    There are over 20 Madison Counties in the U.S., with prominent ones in Alabama (a fast-growing, tech-centric hub with over 388,000 residents) and New York (known for agriculture and a 2020 population of 68,016). They are generally named after President James Madison. 
    Key Madison Counties: 
    • Madison County, Alabama (Huntsville/Madison): Known as a major technology hub, home to the U.S. Space & Rocket Center, Alabama A&M University, and a rapidly expanding population (second-most populous in AL).
    • Madison County, New York (Wampsville): Centered on agriculture, education (Colgate University, Morrisville State College), and landmarks like Cazenovia and Chittenango Falls
      .
    • Madison County, North Carolina: Known for its rural, scenic landscape near Asheville, offering outdoor activities like hot springs, hiking, and farm-to-table dining.
    • Madison County, Florida: Focuses on manufacturing and transportation with access to I-10. 
    Common Characteristics: 
    • County Seats: Examples include Wampsville (NY), Huntsville (AL), Madison (FL), and Virginia City (MT).
    • Economy: Mix of manufacturing, agriculture, and high-tech defense/engineering (specifically in Alabama).
    • Culture: Often known for historical sites, state parks, and rural beauty, such as in North Carolina.
    • Literature/Film: Often associated with the setting for The Bridges of Madison County (typically referred to as Iowa).
