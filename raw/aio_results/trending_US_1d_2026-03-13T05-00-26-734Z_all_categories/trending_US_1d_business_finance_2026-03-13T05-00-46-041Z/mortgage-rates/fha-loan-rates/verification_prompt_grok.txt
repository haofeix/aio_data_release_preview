What is an FHA loan? FHA loans are mortgages backed by the Federal Housing Administration (FHA), an arm of the federal government. “Backed” means the government insures your lender for part of your loan. So your lender will get some of its money back in case of loan default. This insurance, often referred to as the FHA ‘guarantee,’ lets lenders approve FHA loans for borrowers with only fair credit and a relatively small down payment. It’s why these home loans are so popular with first-time buyers and those who have issues in their credit history. FHA mortgage requirements Of course, lenders won’t approve just anyone.
