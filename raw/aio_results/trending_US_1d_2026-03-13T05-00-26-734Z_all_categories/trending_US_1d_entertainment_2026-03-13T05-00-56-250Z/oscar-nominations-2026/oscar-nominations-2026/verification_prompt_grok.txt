The exhaustive and exhausting buildup to the Oscars is nearly over and the two movies that have the best shot at winning the top prize — Paul Thomas Anderson’s darkly comedic action-thriller “One Battle After Another” and Ryan Coogler’s genre-defying American horror story “Sinners” — also happen to be the best films released in 2025.
