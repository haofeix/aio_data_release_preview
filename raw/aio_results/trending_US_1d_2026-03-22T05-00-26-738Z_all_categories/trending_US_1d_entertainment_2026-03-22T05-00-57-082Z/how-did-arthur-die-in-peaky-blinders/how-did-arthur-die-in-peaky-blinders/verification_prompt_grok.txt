Thorne Elizabeth Younger Forum Arthur Shelby Arthur Shelby Jr. is one of the main protagonists of the Peaky Blinders. He is the eldest son of Arthur and Mrs Shelby, older brother of Thomas, John, Ada and Finn Shelby. Arthur served alongside his brothers Thomas and John during World War I; his experiences during it caused him to suffer constant fits of rage upon returning home, making him a dangerous member of the Peaky Blinders. During the rise to power of the Peaky Blinders, Arthur acted as second-in-command to his brother Thomas, while the latter dealt with rival gangs, such as the Birmingham Boys, led by Billy Kimber, or the Italian mafia of London headed by Darby Sabini; all of this made to Arthur becoming vice-president of Shelby Company Limited, which he and his two brothers founded, and a member of the ICA, although it also caused Arthur to develop drug addiction problems which worsened until he met a woman named Linda, whom he married, and had a son named Billy. Arthur temporarily left the Peaky Blinders when one of Thomas's schemes nearly resulted in him, John, and his cousin Michael, Polly's son, being hanged, but he returned to the gangster world when his brother John was killed by New York mobsters who were carrying out a vendetta against the Shelby family; tt was during this time that Arthur relapsed into drug use, a vice that he passed on to his wife Linda. When the vedetta against the New York Mafia was won, Arthur went on to become chief executive of Shelby Company Limited when his brother Thomas became a politician and a member of the Labour Party. Arthur Shelby Jr. was born the 14 March of the years 1895, in a Romani and Irish Traveller family, his parents were Arthur Shelby Sr., a leader of a local gang of Birmingham called the Peaky Blinders, and the Mrs. Shelby, Arthur also had a aunt, Polly Gray. As a child, he and his younger brother John had as their teacher Mrs. Audrey Changretta, the wife of a boss of the Sicilian mafia, with whom his family had a good relationship; Audrey liked Arthur's smile, and usually turned a blind eye to the trouble Arthur got into. Personality[] Arthur is a multifaceted character whose unwavering loyalty to his family drives him to commit brutal and vicious acts. Despite his deep remorse over many of these actions, he often struggles to control his impulses. His short temper and tendency to become easily aggravated, particularly when he perceives an attack on his or his family's pride, further complicate his internal struggles. Throughout the series, Arthur's vulnerability becomes apparent in various situations, all of which stem from the actions of those he loves or cares about. His father's betrayal and his wife's departure are prime examples of events that expose his emotional fragility. Relationships[] Thomas Shelby[] Thomas is Arthur's younger brother, and they share a complex yet close relationship. Despite being the eldest of the Shelby brothers, Arthur does not lead the Peaky Blinders, a fact that often creates tension between the two siblings, with Arthur frequently striving to prove himself to Tommy. Nevertheless, Tommy demonstrates his trust in Arthur by purchasing their local pub, the Garrison, and placing him in charge of its operations. However, the relationship between Thomas and Arthur becomes strained when Arthur attempts to commit suicide on multiple occasions, driven by his belief that he is a failure. While Tommy shows concern for his brother's well-being, he responds to Arthur's failed suicide attempts with laughter, sardonically advising him to do it properly the next time. Arthur Shelby Sr.[] Arthur and his father initially shared a close yet similarly complex relationship. Among the Shelby family and the Peaky Blinders as a whole after he had abandoned them as children, leaving them under the care of their aunt. While Tommy and Polly are hesitant to accept him, Arthur is one of the few who welcome his father back but finds himself entangled in his father's schemes to siphon money from the Peaky Blinders for his personal gain. Arthur Sr. deceived his son into believing they would establish a casino business together, only to abscond with the £500 (equivalent to approximately £6,500 in present-day value). Such betrayal led him to confront his father, who revealed his true nature by threatening to slit his throat if he didn't let him go; this drove Arthur to attempt suicide. Years later, when Arthur and his siblings learned of their father's death, Arthur remained silent, showing no emotion whatsoever, demonstrating that he now hated his father and that his death meant nothing to him; Arthur didn't even name his unborn child after his father. Age Contradiction[] In the movie, the character is described as being younger than in the series and is assumed to have been born in 1895; however, there is also the possibility that the producers misrepresented the timeline. Since they were of Romani origin, it is possible that their birth dates were never officially recorded, and they may have exaggerated their ages in order to participate in the war. However, if we accept the siblings’ ages as shown in the movie, his brother John Shelby would have joined the Great War at 15 and would likely have already been the father of four before even going to war. Given the historical context, this could be possible, but it is also possible that the ages in the movie were given incorrectly. Furthermore, if we accept the ages given in the movie, Thomas Shelby cannot be nine years younger than Freddie Thorne—his schoolmate in the series—who was born in 1889. Either Freddie would have to have been born in the late 1890s, or Thomas Shelby would have to have been born in the early 1890s, which further supports the possibility that the dates were given incorrectly. In the series, Arthur Shelby is known to have been born in 1887. The same debated issue applies to Thomas and John.
