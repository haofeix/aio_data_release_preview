Before the Billiken was Saint Louis University's mascot, he was a national sensation in the early 1900s. Florence Pretz, a Missouri art teacher and illustrator, patented her Billiken design in 1908. It is believed that Pretz was inspired by “The Rubaiyat,” a well-known poem at the time, to create the elfin figure. It is not confirmed whether the firm acquired its name from the Billiken or whether it was an established name before the company bought the rights to Pretz's invention. When did the Billiken become SLU's mascot? The Billiken mascot today. No one is sure exactly when the Billiken first became linked to SLU. A statue of the Billiken sits outside SLU's Chaifetz Arena. Rub his belly for good luck. Where else is the Billiken found? SLU is the only university to claim the Billiken as its mascot.
