Background and coverage breakdown[edit] Logo used until 2018. On April 22, 2010, the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) reached a 14-year agreement,[1][4] worth US$10.8 billion,[5] with CBS and the Turner Broadcasting System to receive joint broadcast rights to the Division I men's college basketball tournament. This came after speculation that ESPN would try to obtain the rights to future tournament games.[6] The NCAA took advantage of an opt-out clause in its 1999 deal with CBS (which ran through 2013, even though the NCAA had the option of ending the agreement after the 2010 championship) to announce its intention to sign a new contract with CBS and Turner Sports, a division of WarnerMedia, which later was absorbed into Warner Bros.
