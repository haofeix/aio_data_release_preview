It was originally scheduled for March 21, 2026, at the Kingdom Arena in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, as part of Riyadh Season, comprising three teams of eight current and former NFL players (including Brady) facing off in a round-robin tournament, with the top two teams advancing to a championship game.
