In many ways Cook was the opposite of Jobs' charismatic leadership style, carving instead a reputation for himself as a calm and mild-mannered leader. Cook leaned into his operations experience, expanding and transforming supply chains and launching a line of Apple-designed chips for the company's devices. Cook also moved Apple beyond hardware into services, introducing a slate of subscription products including Apple News, Apple TV and Apple Pay.
